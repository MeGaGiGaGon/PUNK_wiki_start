# Glands
[[Caps#Glands|Glands]] are the resource used for buying new [[Caps]] upgrades from the [[M-Shop]]

It can be found by:
- Breaking Cacti, dropping 1 [[Caps#Glands|Gland]] pickup and 10-15 [[Caps]] pickups

# Using [[Caps]]
[[Caps]] can be consumed when:
- Firing [[Caps]]-based or [[Caps]]-augmented weapons
- Taking damage on a [[Caps Shield]]

Gel [[Modifiers]], [[Weapons]], and [[Gadgets]] can all be acquired by:
- Purchasing from the [[M-Shop]] for a combination of [[Money]] and [[Caps#Glands|Glands]]
- Breaking [[Crates#White|White]], [[Crates#Caps|Caps]], or [[Crates#Generator|Generator]] [[Crates]]

# Regeneration
[[Caps]] do not regenerate naturally by default

You can gain passive generation using [[Caps Regen]] [[Modifiers]]

Sources of additional [[Caps]] are:
- Breaking Sage, dropping 2-3 [[Caps]] pickups
- Breaking Maracuja, dropping 3-6 [[Caps]] pickups
- Breaking Cacti, dropping 10-15 [[Caps]] pickups and 1 [[Caps#Glands|Glands]] pickup
