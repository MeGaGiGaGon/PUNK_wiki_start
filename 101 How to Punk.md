# General Tips
- All [[Resources]] besides [[Stamina]] do not automatically regenerate, so you should conserve them properly
- All [[Weapons]] have a damaged to cost ratio not stated in-game that you should learn/calculate
- Especially early game, a [[Weapons|Weapon's]] consistency in dealing damage can be more important than the raw numbers
- [[Modifiers]] increase in price after every purchase
- [[Items]] do not increase in price
- Building around available [[Modifiers|Modifier]] connection shapes is extremely important
- [[Booster Core]]s stack with on-[[Modules Grid]] boosts (the plus icons)
- [[Booster Core]]s, [[Extra Projectile]]s, [[Weapons]], and all [[Gadgets]] besides [[Drones]] cannot be boosted
- [[Booster Core]]s are the most powerful [[Modifiers]] in the game, use them wisely
- [[Power Core]]s can be placed anywhere on the [[Modules Grid]] as long as they have a direct connection to a [[Body]]/[[Weapons|Weapon]]/[[Gadgets|Gadget]] slot
- [[Power Core]]s powering area does not need to touch/overlap any other powered area
- Connecting far [[Power Core]]s is a good use for [[Modifiers]]/[[Upgrades]] you are not using
- [[Loadouts]] are unlocked by finding their corresponding weapon in a run
- [[Drones]] will not target [[Station Mimic]]s
- [[Drones]] will always target nearby [[Crates]] and [[Glands]], even if you may have wanted to save them for later
- Some [[Weapons]] are not affected by some [[Modifiers]], though any passive effects the [[Modifiers]] have will still apply
- [[Drones]] always start with the full [[Resources]] given, but do not consume any to construct besides the listed count of their primary [[Resources|Resource]]
- [[Drones]] are affected by [[Modifiers]] that target the [[Weapons|Weapon]] they use
- [[Extra Projectile]] works best with low base projectile count [[Weapons]], as long as you can handle the spread
- [[Extra Projectile]] will completely stop you from firing if you run out of the corresponding [[Resources|Resource]]
