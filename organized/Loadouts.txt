There are 6 loadouts, 1 always unlocked and 5 unlockable.

The 5 unloackable loadouts are unlocked by equiping their corresponding primary weapon in a run, and stays unlocked between runs.

Besides the starting weapon, there is no difference between the different loadouts

The loadouts are:
* Gunner ([[Popper]]) (Always unlocked)
* Rocketeer ([[Dart]])
* Slayer ([[Blades]])
* Rusher ([[Shotgun]])
* Thrower ([[Debris]])
* Wormster ([[Worm]])
