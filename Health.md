[[Health]] is the most important resource

When you run out of [[Health]], you die and the run ends

# Regeneration
[[Health]] does not regenerate naturally

You can gain passive generation using [[Health Regen]] [[Modifiers]]

Sources of additional [[Health]] are:
- Breaking Bark, dropping 1 [[Health]] pickup at 15% chance
- Breaking [[Vein Fruit]]s, dropping 3 [[Health]] pickups
- Breaking [[Boxes#Health|Health Boxes]], dropping 10 [[Health]] pickups
