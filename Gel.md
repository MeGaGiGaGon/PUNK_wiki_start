# Fiber
[[Gel#Fiber|Fiber]] is the resource used for buying new [[Gel]] upgrades from the [[M-Shop]]

It can be found by:
- Breaking Giant Buds, dropping 1 [[Gel#Fiber|Fiber]] pickup and 10-15 [[Gel]] pickups

# Using [[Gel]]
[[Gel]] can be consumed when:
- Firing [[Gel]]-based or [[Gel]]-augmented weapons
- Taking damage on a [[Gel Shield]]

Gel [[Modifiers]], [[Weapons]], and [[Gadgets]] can all be acquired by:
- Purchasing from the [[M-Shop]] for a combination of [[Money]] and [[Gel#Fiber|Fiber]]
- Breaking [[Crates#White|White]], [[Crates#Purple|Purple]], or [[Crates#Generator|Generator]] [[Crates]]

# Regeneration
[[Gel]] does not regenerate naturally by default

You can gain passive generation using [[Gel Regen]] [[Modifiers]]

Sources of additional [[Gel]] are:
- Breaking Small Buds, dropping 1-2 [[Gel]] pickups
- Breaking Large Buds, dropping 2-3 [[Gel]] pickups
- Breaking Giant Buds, dropping 10-15 [[Gel]] pickups and 1 [[Gel#Fiber|Fiber]] pickup
