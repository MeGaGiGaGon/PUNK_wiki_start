The infinite fuel is only provided when missing one or more fuel sections

A consistent way to ensure max fuel is to [[Movement#Dash|Dash]] in place, as [[Movement#Dash|Dashing]] still consumes [[Fuel]] even when not moving

[[Stations]] besides the starting one must be opened before they can be used

Opened [[Stations]] can be traveled between using the [[Map]] when at any station

[[Stations]] do not protect against any forms of damage when traveling, and during the opening animation the player can take several damage or fully die before control is retained

For this reason it is highly recommended that you do not open stations in the middle of a hazard like [[Gas]] or [[Lightning Poles]] as it can be very easy to accidentally travel to them hours later

When near a station, the player can access the [[I-Shop]], [[M-Shop]], travel between opened stations, and edit the [[Modules Grid]]
